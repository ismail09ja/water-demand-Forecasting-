DATASET DESCRIPTION

The dataset used in this project consists of 1,827 synthetic records representing daily water demand data for an urban area from 2020-01-01 to 2024-12-31. Each record contains details about environmental conditions, demographic factors, time-based features, and historical demand patterns that influence daily water consumption.

This dataset reflects real-world patterns of water demand including seasonal variations, weekly cycles, weather impacts, and population growth trends, making it suitable for building accurate forecasting models.

Feature Name                    Description

date                            Daily date (2020-01-01 to 2024-12-31)

temperature                     Daily temperature in Celsius (seasonal pattern with noise)

rainfall                        Daily rainfall in millimeters (seasonal pattern with random events)

population_index                Population growth index (gradual upward trend with 2% annual growth)

water_demand                    Daily water demand in liters (Target Variable)

day                             Day of the month (1-31)

month                           Month of the year (1-12)

day_of_week                     Day of week (0=Monday, 6=Sunday)

day_of_year                     Day of year (1-365/366)

week                            Week number of the year

quarter                         Quarter of the year (1-4)

year                            Year (2020-2024)

month_sin                       Cyclical encoding of month (sine component)

month_cos                       Cyclical encoding of month (cosine component)

day_of_week_sin                 Cyclical encoding of day of week (sine component)

day_of_week_cos                 Cyclical encoding of day of week (cosine component)

day_of_year_sin                 Cyclical encoding of day of year (sine component)

day_of_year_cos                 Cyclical encoding of day of year (cosine component)

demand_lag_1                    Water demand from 1 day ago

demand_lag_7                    Water demand from 7 days ago (weekly pattern)

demand_lag_30                   Water demand from 30 days ago (monthly pattern)

demand_rolling_mean_7           Rolling average of water demand over 7 days

demand_rolling_mean_30          Rolling average of water demand over 30 days

demand_rolling_std_7            Rolling standard deviation of water demand over 7 days

Target Variable

water_demand → Continuous numerical value representing daily water consumption in liters

This is a time series regression problem.

The project aims to forecast future daily water demand based on historical patterns, weather conditions, and demographic trends.

Using machine learning and time series models, the system analyzes temperature, rainfall, population growth, seasonal patterns, weekly cycles, and historical demand to predict future water consumption.

The output helps water management authorities plan infrastructure capacity, optimize distribution systems, and implement demand-side management strategies to ensure sustainable water supply.
